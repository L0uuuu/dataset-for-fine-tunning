WHAT MAKES A "COMPLEX SCENARIO" (vs. a simple Q&A)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Each scenario must have ALL of the following characteristics:

1. NARRATIVE SITUATION (not a question)
   The user tells a story: "Je suis gérant d'une SARL depuis 3 ans. Mon associé
   majoritaire veut me révoquer sans motif. En même temps, j'ai découvert qu'il
   a signé un contrat avec un fournisseur sans mon accord, engageant la société
   pour 500 000 DT..."

2. MULTIPLE INTERTWINED LEGAL ISSUES (minimum 3)
   Each scenario must touch at least 3 distinct legal questions that naturally
   arise from the same situation. These should span different chapters/sections
   of the code when possible.
   Examples of intertwined issues:
   - Validity of a contract + liability for breach + damages calculation
   - Manager dismissal + scope of authority + third-party protection
   - Company formation defects + partner obligations + dissolution grounds

3. REQUIRES 4–8 ARTICLES to answer properly
   The assistant's answer must synthesize multiple articles. The retrieved context
   should include 4–8 relevant articles.

4. REALISTIC PERSONA
   Each scenario must come from a believable Tunisian persona:
   - A small business owner in Sfax dealing with a partner dispute
   - An HR director in Tunis handling a complex employment situation
   - A citizen who inherited property and faces contract issues
   - A startup founder confused about corporate governance
   - A tenant/landlord in a commercial lease dispute
   Do NOT write sterile textbook hypotheticals. Include realistic details:
   specific amounts in DT (Tunisian Dinars), city names, industry context,
   emotional undertones ("je suis inquiet", "أنا قلق من...").

5. SOME ISSUES THE CODE ANSWERS, SOME IT DOESN'T
   At least one sub-issue in each scenario should be something the uploaded code
   does NOT fully address — forcing the assistant to:
   - Answer what it can from the retrieved articles
   - Explicitly say which part it cannot answer from the provided documents

ANSWER STRUCTURE (for the assistant response)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The assistant's answer must follow this structure:

(a) ACKNOWLEDGE THE SITUATION
    Brief empathetic acknowledgment (1–2 sentences). Not robotic.
    "Je comprends la complexité de votre situation. Plusieurs questions
    juridiques se posent ici."

(b) ISSUE-BY-ISSUE ANALYSIS
    Break down the scenario into distinct legal issues. For each issue:
    - Name the issue clearly (as a subheading in the answer text)
    - State the applicable legal principle with article citation [1], [2], etc.
    - Note relevant exceptions or conditions from the code
    - Apply it briefly to the user's specific facts

(c) LIMITS OF THE RESPONSE
    Clearly identify what the provided documents do NOT cover:
    "Concernant [specific sub-issue], les documents fournis ne contiennent pas
    de dispositions applicables. Cette question relève de [other code/authority]."

(d) PRACTICAL NEXT STEPS
    Concrete, actionable recommendations:
    "Je vous recommande de : (1) ... (2) ... (3) consulter un avocat spécialisé
    en droit des sociétés pour les aspects non couverts ci-dessus."

(e) SOURCES LINE
    "Sources : art. X, Y, Z, W du [Code abbreviation]"

(f) DISCLAIMER
    French: "Ceci ne constitue pas un avis juridique. Pour votre situation
    spécifique, consultez un avocat."
